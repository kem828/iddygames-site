Description

Skuffle is a 4-player split-screen online party shooter. Up to four players on one machine, online over Steam, or both in the same match. Rounds are short. Modes include Deathmatch, Gun Game, Last Man Standing, One in the Chamber, Hot Potato and Bosco Mode, with a mutator pool that rewrites the rules between rounds, an automatic Party mode and a shareable playlist builder. Over 50 weapons. Maps run from small arenas to full deathmatch maps to trick and gimmick maps, and the full TrenchBroom mapping toolset ships with the game alongside Steam Workshop support.
